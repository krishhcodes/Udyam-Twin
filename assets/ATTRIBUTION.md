# Asset Attributions & Licences

## 1. 3D Geometry
- **Asset:** Representative 3-axis CNC Vertical Machining Center (VMC)
- **Source Project:** [TseNC](https://github.com/fab-machines/TseNC)
- **Institution:** Rhine-Waal University of Applied Sciences (FabLab Kamp-Lintfort)
- **Licence:** CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 — Strongly Reciprocal (CERN-OHL-S) / Weakly Reciprocal (CERN-OHL-W)
- **Modifications:** Fasteners removed, simplified into functional kinematic groups (`spindle`, `head`, `table`, `column`, `base`, `workpiece`), decimated for WebGL browser rendering.
- **Operating Envelope Note:** Operating speeds, torque, power, and thermal dynamics follow the AI4I 2020 industrial machining dataset, not TseNC's desktop router specifications.

## 2. Predictive Maintenance Dataset
- **Dataset:** AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset
- **Authors:** Stephan Matzka, School of Engineering — Technology and Life, HTW Berlin
- **Source:** UC Irvine Machine Learning Repository (DOI: 10.24432/C5HS5C)
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Usage:** Synthetic ground-truth baseline for tool wear, torque, rotational speed, and failure classification.

## 3. Grid Emission Factor
- **Source:** Central Electricity Authority (CEA), Ministry of Power, Government of India
- **Publication:** CO₂ Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector (User Guide Version 20.0, 2024–2025)
- **Weighted Average Emission Factor:** 0.716 kg CO₂ / kWh (National Grid)
